# Utopia Group Website — V4

A responsive, motion-enhanced static multi-page corporate website for Utopia Group, based on the supplied 2026 Company Profile and the user's approved website sitemap/design direction.

## Design direction
- Brand-led hero composition
- Modern sans-serif typography
- White, black, minimal grey, with restrained red accent
- Mixed brand presentation using typographic wordmarks and abstract editorial visual panels
- Responsive desktop/mobile navigation
- OUR BRANDS dropdown plus a dedicated brand directory
- Individual page for each of the seven requested brands
- News & Insights and Social Media are separate
- Social directory includes Instagram and TikTok only; no YouTube

## Deploy to Vercel
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload the contents to the root of the GitHub repository (not the ZIP itself).
3. Ensure the homepage filename is exactly `index.html` (lowercase).
4. Commit changes to the `main` branch.
5. Vercel should redeploy automatically. If not, open Deployments and redeploy the latest commit.
6. Framework preset: Other. No build command is required.

## Source fidelity and content notes
- Company overview, vision, mission, values, leadership, capabilities, portfolio classification, brand descriptions, timeline, growth strategy, presence/expansion, partnership categories, and office contact details are based on the uploaded Utopia Group Company Profile 2026.
- The seven-brand website order follows the user's instruction: Hanam BBQ, Samyuga Korean BBQ, Butatei Japanese Butadon, Shaolin Malatang, Hi Kims!, Banco Artisan Bakery, Oppa Dosirak.
- The PDF classifies Hanam BBQ and Samyuga as Primary Growth; Shaolin Malatang and Butatei as Secondary Growth; Hi Kims! and Banco Artisan Bakery as Other Brands. Oppa Dosirak appears in the company timeline (2019) but is not assigned a portfolio growth tier in the PDF.
- Brand-specific gallery images, product/menu details, verified outlet links, Instagram/TikTok URLs, and official group social accounts were not present in the PDF. Their spaces are prepared, but official assets and links must be supplied before launch.
- News articles are clearly marked as editorial previews, not verified announcements.
- Contact and partnership forms are demo-only and do not transmit or store submissions until connected to a form service.
- Brand visuals are typographic/abstract placeholders, not official logos or product photographs.
