UTOPIA.

Utopia Group is an Indonesian food and beverage group focused on developing and operating distinctive culinary brands across diverse dining segments. Its approach brings brand development, sourcing, production, restaurant operations, marketing, and business systems together to support consistent experiences and disciplined growth.
